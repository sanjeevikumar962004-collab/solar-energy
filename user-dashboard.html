Frequently Asked Questions

How does net energy metering true-up work with PGE?

Under Oregon NEM 3.0 regulations, surplus kilowatt-hours exported by your solar system during daylight hours are credited to your account at retail value. Every October, PGE calculates your cumulative annual balance in a true-up settlement.

What happens during a neighborhood grid outage?

With your Tesla Powerwall 3 integration, microgrid isolation triggers automatically within 10 milliseconds. Your essential home circuits remain powered continuously from stored battery power and active daytime solar generation.

How often do solar panels need professional cleaning?

In Oregon, seasonal rains naturally clear light dust. However, an annual spring wash and micro-inverter diagnostic by our certified technicians is recommended to preserve the 98%+ peak efficiency warranty.
