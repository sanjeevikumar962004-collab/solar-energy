Frequently Asked Questions

Modern bifacial and N-Type TOPCon cells capture diffuse infrared and UV radiation that passes straight through marine overcast clouds. In Salem, our installations consistently generate 35% to 45% of peak summer capacity even on rainy December days, which is supplemented seamlessly by our battery reserves.
Under Oregon net metering rules, when your solar array produces more electricity than your home consumes, the excess is sent back into the local grid and spins your meter backward, earning you 1:1 billing credits that you use at night or during winter months.
The Federal Residential Clean Energy Credit (Section 25D) allows homeowners to deduct 30% of the total installation and battery storage cost directly dollar-for-dollar from their federal taxes. Stackly provides all itemized paperwork and certified engineering affidavits for effortless filing.
With a Stackly PowerCore or paired battery storage system, an automatic microsecond transfer switch disconnects your home from the utility grid to protect line workers, instantly islanding your home so your solar panels keep running and powering your essentials without interruption.
