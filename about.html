Who We Are

We don't just bolt panels on roofs. We engineer smart energy ecosystems.

Born in Salem in 2018, Stackly started with five electrical engineers who asked a simple question: Why is modern home energy still chained to vulnerable, fossil-fueled grids?

Oregon has abundant natural energy. By pairing ultra-high-density bifacial solar arrays with intelligent battery storage and local telemetry, we transform single-family homes and Salem businesses into resilient, self-generating micro-utilities.
